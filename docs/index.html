AlreadyOwned

After a flood, a fire or a break-in, the insurer wants proof you owned what you lost. The proof is usually sitting in your camera roll. Walk the room, say what you see, and this add-on is built to find it.

See the claim it writes Watch a practice run

Boxed in green: taken before the loss, kept as proof. Crossed out: taken after, kept as a damage photo only. It also shows you what you forgot you owned.

On my real claim, 22 of the 27 things I lost were already in photos I had taken. Searching the same library by file name found 0.

The 22 is counted by hand, photo by photo, over the 1,769 photos my phone and PC held from before the flood. A phone names a photo 20251211_160058.jpg, and it never says sofa. With a small vision model on my PC standing in for the NAS, the add-on's own search proved 13 of the 22, and found one my hand count had missed. Its count on the NAS itself is the first thing I measure there.

One folder you can hand to the insurer

A row for every item, the photo that proves it, the receipt if there is one, and a plain "no proof" where there is none.
