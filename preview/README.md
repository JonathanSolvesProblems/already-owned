# Preview images

Eight images for the project page gallery, 1500 x 1000, in upload order. Built by `tools/build_preview.py`: the figures are read from the results files and the captures are taken from the live page. Paste each caption into the gallery's caption field (Devpost allows 140 characters; each is under).

1. `01-cover.png`

   22 of the 27 things I lost in a flood were already in my photos. Already Owned, a Skill for UGREEN AI NAS, finds them for the insurer.

2. `02-baseline.png`

   My real claim: 22 of 27 items appear in pre-loss photos (hand count). The Skill's search proved 13 of them; file names and dates proved 0.

3. `03-voice-note.png`

   Walk the room with a voice note. The NAS transcribes it and looks for each thing you named in photos from before the loss. (Practice run)

4. `04-what-you-forgot.png`

   Then it shows things in the same photos that you never named, and says plainly what it cannot find. (Practice run, made-up library)

5. `05-claim-folder.png`

   One claim folder: a proof level on every row, read back after saving, and each exhibit with its capture time and SHA-256. (Sample)

6. `06-what-it-refuses.png`

   It documents what exists. It will not alter a receipt, count a photo from after the loss, guess a value or swap in a similar item.

7. `07-how-it-is-built.png`

   Five of the NAS's six expert agents, by delegation only. Every write waits for approval; nothing is deleted, moved or overwritten.

8. `08-rehearsed.png`

   A small model played the NAS assistant against a scripted NAS: 140 of 140 checks passed. Recall on a real NAS is not measured yet.
